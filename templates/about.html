About StudyPlanner

A Production-Grade Academic Time Management System

Project Mission

StudyPlanner is engineered specifically for students looking to optimize their study hours, manage coursework deadlines, and maintain focus. Built with Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, and modern CSS3, it offers an intuitive workflow for academic success.

Key Capabilities

  • Task Organization: Assign priority tiers (High, Medium, Low) and monitor progress (Pending, In Progress, Completed).
  • Integrated Stopwatch Timer: Accurately log focus sessions and automatically aggregate total study hours.
  • Dynamic Search & Filtering: Instantly filter tasks by status, priority, or search keywords with smart pagination.
  • Enterprise Security: Built with Flask-WTF CSRF protection, Flask-Login user sessions, Flask-Limiter rate protection, and Werkzeug password hashing.

Technology Stack

Python 3.10 Flask Framework SQLAlchemy ORM SQLite3 WTForms HTML5 & Vanilla CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript Render.com
