# StudyPlanner User Manual

**Course:** CSCD602: Advanced Software Engineering  
**Institution:** University of Ghana, Department of Computer Science  
**Project:** StudyPlanner Application  

---

## 1. System Access & Registration

### 1.1 Accessing the Application
Navigate to the live application URL in your web browser:
`https://studyplanner-capstone.onrender.com`

### 1.2 Creating an Account
1. Click the **Get Started** button on the homepage navigation bar.
2. Fill in your details on the registration page:
   - **Username**: Choose a unique handle (3-64 characters, letters, numbers, underscores).
   - **Email**: Enter your university or personal email address (`name@domain.edu`).
   - **Password**: Enter a password (minimum 8 characters, containing both letters and numbers).
   - **Confirm Password**: Re-enter password.
3. Click **Create Account**. You will be logged in automatically and redirected to your dashboard.

---

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

Upon logging in, your dashboard presents an overview of your academic workload:
- **Total Tasks**: Count of all active and completed tasks.
- **Pending Tasks**: Work not yet started.
- **In Progress**: Tasks actively being worked on.
- **Completed**: Finished coursework.
- **Total Time Studied**: Cumulative hours and minutes logged using the Study Timer.
- **Priority Tasks Table**: Quick view of upcoming deadlines and High-priority items.

---

## 3. Managing Study Tasks

### 3.1 Creating a New Task
1. Click **+ Create New Task** from the top navigation bar or dashboard.
2. Enter the task details:
   - **Task Title**: Name of the assignment or topic (e.g., *Read Chapter 4 Systems Programming*).
   - **Description**: Optional notes, chapter breakdowns, or links.
   - **Due Date & Time**: Set the assignment deadline.
   - **Priority**: Select **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**.
   - **Status**: Select **Pending**, **In Progress**, or **Completed**.
3. Click **Create Task**.

### 3.2 Searching and Filtering Tasks
1. Click **My Tasks** in the navigation bar.
2. Use the filter bar to:
   - Type keywords into the **Search Tasks** field.
   - Filter by **Status** or **Priority** dropdowns.
   - Sort by **Due Date**, **Priority**, or **Title**.
3. Click **Apply Filters**.

### 3.3 Editing and Deleting Tasks
- **Edit**: Click the ✏️ **Edit** button on any task card to update its title, status, or priority.
- **Delete**: Click the 🗑️ **Delete** button. A confirmation popup will prompt you to confirm deletion.

---

## 4. Operating the Study Timer

1. On any task card or detail page, click ⏱️ **Study Timer**.
2. On the Timer page, click ▶️ **Start Study Session**. The clock will tick in real time (`HH:MM:SS`).
3. Focus on your coursework.
4. When finished, click ⏹️ **Stop & Save Session**. The system will calculate your exact focus time, log the session, and update your cumulative study hours.

---

## 5. Logging Out
Click **Log Out** in the upper-right user menu to safely terminate your session.
